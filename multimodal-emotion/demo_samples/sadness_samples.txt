SADNESS TEXT SAMPLES
====================

Sample 1 (Grief):
"My grandfather passed away last week. I miss him so much and wish I could talk to him one more time."

Sample 2 (Loneliness):
"I feel so alone right now. Nobody understands what I'm going through and I have no one to talk to."

Sample 3 (Disappointment):
"I worked so hard for this and it didn't work out. I feel like I let everyone down, including myself."

Sample 4 (Heartbreak):
"They left without saying goodbye. Everything reminds me of them and it hurts so much."

Sample 5 (Despair):
"I don't know what to do anymore. Everything feels hopeless and empty. Nothing makes sense."

Sample 6 (Regret):
"I wish I had done things differently. Now it's too late and I can't fix my mistakes."

Sample 7 (Melancholy):
"Looking at old photos makes me realize how much has changed. Those happy times feel so far away now."

Sample 8 (Abandoned):
"Everyone moved on with their lives and forgot about me. I feel invisible and unwanted."
